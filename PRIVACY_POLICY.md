# Privacy Policy for Drape

Last updated: [DATE]

**What Drape stores (on your phone only)**
Your body measurements and proportions, a small face crop you choose, your closet list, saved looks, wear history and settings. This data stays on your device. You can delete all of it any time from Saved → Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the app. If you create a backup file, you control where it goes. Backups never include your API key.

**Photos**
Photos you take or choose are used only when you tap Analyze or Scan. At that moment the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to the AI service you configured (Anthropic's Claude API, or your own server) to read outlines, colours and clothing. Drape does not save your original photos. Anthropic's handling of API data is described in its own privacy policy.

**Location (optional)**
If you turn it on, Drape uses your approximate location, or a city you pick, to request today's weather from Open-Meteo, and may look up the place name through BigDataCloud. Coordinates are sent to those services only to get the weather and place name. Location is not stored on any server by Drape. You can turn it off in Settings or Android Settings.

**Camera, photos and notifications**
Camera access is used only to take photos you choose. Photo access uses Android's photo picker, so Drape only sees photos you select. Notifications are used only for your daily look reminder.

**Sharing and ads**
Drape has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and does not sell data.

**Children**
Drape is not directed at children under 13.
