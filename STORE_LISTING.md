# Play Store listing (copy and paste)

**App name:** Drape: 3D Wardrobe & Daily Looks
**Short description (80 chars):** See dresses on a 3D you, count your closet, get a new look daily.
**Full description:**
Drape builds a 3D figure of you from your own photo, then helps you decide what to wear.

• Fit your 3D model from a full-length photo and map your real face onto it
• Try dress styles, necklines, sleeves, lengths and colours on your 3D you
• Get an honest score and tips on what suits your colouring and shape
• Photograph your shelves and let AI count every piece in your closet
• Get a fresh outfit idea every day from the clothes you already own
• Local weather sets the look automatically (optional location)
• Daily reminder at the time you choose
• Saved looks, backups, and one-tap delete of all your data

Your photos are never stored by the app. AI features need an internet connection.
